wins.

## Free, no licence needed

- Making decks for yourself, your employer or your clients, including paid client work.
- Using SlideCraft inside a company, any size, on any number of machines.
- Changing the code, adding layouts, themes and storylines, and using your changes internally.
- Building SlideCraft into your own workflow: CI, internal tools, agents, scripts.
- Sharing copies, changed or not, with the licence and its `Required Notice:` lines attached.

The decks you make are yours, for any purpose.

## Needs a commercial licence

Anything that competes with SlideCraft, for example:

- a hosted service that makes decks for other people or companies with SlideCraft;
- reselling SlideCraft, or a white-label version of it;
- embedding SlideCraft in a presentation, document or AI product that you sell or offer to others;
- a paid plugin, template pack or marketplace listing built from SlideCraft's code.

Not sure whether your case is covered? Write before you build; you will get an answer in writing.

## What a commercial licence can include

- a written licence for your use case, with an invoice your procurement team can process;
- your corporate PowerPoint template set up as a SlideCraft theme, calibrated and checked;
- custom layouts or storylines for your domain;
- priority answers on issues that block you.

Bundled fonts, icons and libraries keep their own licences ([NOTICE](NOTICE)). The name
"SlideCraft" and its logo are not licensed: a fork needs a different name.
