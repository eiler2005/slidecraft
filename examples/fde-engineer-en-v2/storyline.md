# Storyline — Forward Deployed Engineers turn AI pilots into working products (v2)

## Governing thought
Most AI pilots stall between demo and production; the Forward Deployed Engineer closes that gap where value is made — so leaders should approve a one-quarter pilot with one FDE, a fixed budget and weekly metrics.

## SCR
- **Situation** — Companies run many AI pilots and the tools are widely available.
- **Complication** — Few pilots reach production with measurable value; the gap is integration into real workflows and the organisation around them, not the model.
- **Resolution** — An engineer embedded with the users who builds and ships on the platform, backed by a few organisational changes, tested in one quarter.

## Key line
1. Demand is real and recent: FDE postings rose more than eightfold in 2025 (FT via Fast Company); ~95% of GenAI pilots show no P&L impact (MIT NANDA).
2. In the AI value chain the FDE owns the middle — data access, integration, deployment and adoption — where pilots stall.
3. The work is a repeatable cycle from the user's desk to the core product.
4. The profile is specific: production engineering, applied AI and customer judgement.
5. The organisation must change too: skills, staff placement and incentives are the usual gaps (7S check, illustrative).
6. The pilot is small and measurable: adoption and automation targets per month (illustrative plan) for a fixed budget.
7. Ask: approve the pilot.
