# Storyline — Forward Deployed Engineers turn AI pilots into working products

## Governing thought
Most AI pilots stall between demo and production; the Forward Deployed Engineer closes that gap, so leaders should pilot one FDE on their highest-value use case.

## SCR
- **Situation** — Companies run many AI pilots and the tools are widely available.
- **Complication** — Few pilots reach production with measurable value; the gap is integration into real workflows, not the model.
- **Resolution** — An engineer embedded with the users who builds and ships on the platform: the Forward Deployed Engineer.

## Key line
1. Demand is real and recent: FDE postings rose more than eightfold in 2025 (FT via Fast Company).
2. The role is distinct: it owns adoption in production, unlike product engineers, solutions engineers or consultants.
3. The work is a repeatable cycle from the user's desk to the core product.
4. The profile is specific: production engineering, applied AI and customer judgement.
5. Start small: one FDE, one workflow, one quarter.
