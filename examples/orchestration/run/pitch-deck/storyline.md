# Storyline — Fabrikam turns idle truck space into same-day freight

## Governing thought
Fabrikam fills the 31% of truck capacity that drives empty, and €3M takes it to three countries and break-even in 2028.

## SCR
- **Situation** — Small shippers wait days for part-load freight or pay for full trucks.
- **Complication** — Every third truck on the road drives with empty space; brokers are slow and load boards do not serve small shippers.
- **Resolution** — An instant marketplace that matches loads to trucks already on the route; €3M funds two launches.

## Key line (MECE)
1. The problem is large and unsolved: €14B market, 31% empty capacity.
2. The product works: bookings grew eight-fold in nine months with no paid marketing.
3. The economics scale: 21% take rate, two-month payback.
